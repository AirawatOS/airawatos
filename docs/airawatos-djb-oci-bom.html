Assumptions & exclusions

  • One node, Docker Compose — no OKE, managed PostgreSQL, or load balancer in the pilot (PostgreSQL runs on the VM). Those belong to the later HA/production design and are not in this BOM.
  • Region: an OCI India region for data residency. Prices/availability vary slightly by region.
  • Egress assumed within OCI's 10 TB/month free allowance (the public-data connectors are light).
  • Excludes: one-time migration/setup effort, DJB's own network links/VPN, any third-party data subscriptions, and OCI support plan tier.
  • Software: AirawatOS and all platform components (PostgreSQL, Keycloak, Vault, OpenFGA, nginx) are open-source / self-hosted — no per-seat or license line item.
