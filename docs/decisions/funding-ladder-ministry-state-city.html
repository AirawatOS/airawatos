Instantiating the Ladder — Ministry · State · City

The same funder ↔ implementer edge, chained across three sovereign tenants. Nothing new is invented at each rung: information climbs (need · proposal · report), money descends (program · award · tranche), and the intermediary in the middle is simply both roles at once.

What appears “for free” when you chain the dyad

↑ Need aggregation

City need rolls into a state total, the state total into a national one — exchange 1 chained. Each rung aggregates; raw data never leaves its tenant. The ministry sizes envelopes against live ground truth.

↓ Program cascade

A ministry program flows down; the state may staple on a top-up before passing it to cities — exchange 2 chained. Eligibility and reporting terms travel with the money.

⑤ One report → all funders

The city executes and reports once. MoHUA, HUDD, and the bank each hold a progress subscription on that single project — same tamper-evident numbers, no parallel portals.

⑥ Co-release on one milestone

A verified milestone VC unlocks every funder's next tranche at once — MoHUA its 60%, HUDD its 40%, the bank its RBF slice — each posting in its own ledger. Information moves once; money follows, N-ways.

Disbursement stays human-admitted. The platform supplies the verified precondition (the milestone VC on the subscription); a person at each funder admits the release. The payment rail itself (PFMS / treasury) is an external boundary — this models the decision and the evidence, not the money movement.
