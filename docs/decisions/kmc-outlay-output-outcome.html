4 · What must be built to close the thread

The need half is live; the money is committed but unspent; the delivery→benefit half is the gap. Four artifacts complete the loop:

  1. An output noun — project.output: a delivered unit (a built dwelling, a laid connection) bound to its project, its milestone, its cost (finance.transaction), and its space. This is the missing pivot between money and benefit. ✗
  2. The outlay→output joint — post finance.transactions against outputs so cost-per-unit is a fact, not a spreadsheet. (Ledger governed, currently empty.) ◐
  3. The output→outcome joint — prov.derivation (ADR-0041 lineage): the record that says this output caused that welfare.gap to close. Without it, outcome is correlation, not attribution. ✗
  4. Outcome re-measurement wiring — on milestone completion, re-run the welfare gap engine over the project's cells and record the delta as the outcome against the results contract (Plan-vs-Actual at the outcome level, not just budget). ◐

With these, a funder reading the progress subscription sees the whole thread: ₹ released → units built here → crowding fell there → N people crossed the floor — every link a signed, located, dated, attributed fact.
