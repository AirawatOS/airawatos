Airawat · ADR-0071 / ADR-0072

The Funder ↔ Implementer Interaction

One relationship, applied recursively, is the whole federation. Two roles — a Funding Agency that holds the envelope and a Implementing Agency that does the work — exchange six governed objects. The rule that binds them: money cannot move until information moves.

The end-to-end sequence

Precondition. Both parties are provisioned tenants on the network.jurisdiction ladder, each sovereign over its own data. The Funder holds an envelope (finance.budget); the Implementer runs the welfare spine (ADR-0069) over its own city.
1

Need signal Implementer → Funder

The Implementer publishes its aggregated welfare need — "N people below the crowding floor", "M below the water floor". The Funder subscribes. Only aggregates cross the tenant boundary; raw per-cell / per-household data never leaves. The Funder now sizes envelopes against live ground truth, not a stale survey.

rail · aggregator lanehonest intervals survive the rollup (ADR-0070)
2

Program offer Funder → Implementer

The Funder publishes a funding.program to the directory: the envelope, the eligibility rule, the co-funding structure (its own share + any co-funders), and the reporting terms that will govern release. The matcher surfaces the program to eligible Implementers as candidates — a human picks; the platform never auto-applies.

rail · directory + matcherterms travel with the money
3

Proposal / application Implementer → Funder

The Implementer's officer (the Junior-Engineer feasibility step — the human decision gate) authors a project.proposal: the diagnosis it answers, the site, the chosen intervention lever, a results contract, a costed budget, and the ask. It is submitted to the program over the Case rails (directed) — or listed openly for discovery.

rail · Case rails (ADR-0067)pre-money · rejectable · resubmittable
4

Appraisal & award Funder → Implementer

The Funder appraises in its own Inbox/Case queue. A human sanction is a governance.decision; on approve it issues a funding.award — its share, signed from its tenant, partial-capable so several awards stack. The proposal accrues awards until Σ awards ≥ ask; the award that closes the gap flips proposal → project (the state transition) and is issued as a VC the Implementer holds.

rail · decision spine + VCco-funding = many funder-edges at once
5

Subscription & report Implementer → Funder

The Implementer executes; the Project layer emits receipts, Plan-vs-Actual, and milestone VCs as it goes. Each Funder holds a progress subscription — a governed read-grant on that one project. It reports once; every co-funder sees the same tamper-evident numbers. No parallel portals, no reconciliation.

rail · progress subscription (aggregator lane, reverse)one truth · N subscribers
6

Tranche release Funder → Implementer

The Funder's next instalment is a decided change-request whose precondition is a verified milestone VC on its subscription. On human admit, it posts the release in its own ledger (finance.transaction) and issues a VC receipt. Each co-funder releases its share against the same milestone.

↳ This is exchange 5 gating exchange 6: money cannot move until the information moves.

rail · decision spine + financehuman-admitted · platform supplies the precondition, never moves money

Why it composes — roles are relational

A tenant is not "a funder" or "an implementer" in the abstract — it plays a role per edge. The same body is an implementer facing up and a funder facing down. Chain the one dyad and the jurisdiction ladder appears; nothing new is invented at each rung.

Funder ──▶ Implementer   (ministry → state)
        └─ also Funder ──▶ Implementer   (state → city)
                └─ also Funder ──▶ Beneficiary   (city → citizen)

An intermediary agency (the state) is implementer-facing-up + funder-facing-down: it re-aggregates need upward (exchange 1) and re-packages programs downward (exchange 2). Co-funding is one implementer holding funder-edges to several funders simultaneously — the 60:40 stack of exchange 4.

What this leans on vs. what is new

Reused — already built

  • Jurisdiction ladder + service federation (ADR-0067)
  • Cross-tenant aggregator lane — both directions
  • Decision spine (ADR-0029) + Inbox / Case
  • Project layer — receipts + Plan-vs-Actual
  • Finance core — double-entry ledger
  • Verifiable Credentials (ADR-0049)
  • The internal agency stack is identical for every tenant — role is config

New — to build

  • funding.program co-funding structure
  • funding.award — signed, partial, stacking
  • Funding stack rule — Σ awards ≥ ask
  • Cross-tenant progress subscription
  • Tranche-release gate — release ⇐ verified milestone
  • Hierarchical need aggregation up the ladder
