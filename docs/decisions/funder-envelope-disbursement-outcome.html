Inside the Funding Agency — the Mirror Thread

The funder runs the same chain inverted and at portfolio scale: from an envelope, through awards and gated disbursements, to an outcome measured across every implementer it funds. Its "output" is money released against verified progress; its "outcome" is aggregate need reduction. The two threads meet at exactly two shared objects.

1 · The funding agency's modules — the same edge, inverted

The funder receives need, proposals, and reports (in); it sends programs, awards, and tranches (out) — the exact inverse of the implementer. Its internal modules are the same tenant-generic ERP stack, configured for the funding role.

3 · Where the two threads meet — two shared objects

The implementer thread and the funder thread are not parallel copies — they are joined at exactly two governed objects. This is the whole point of the federation: one object, read by both sides, each in its own role.

The milestone VC is the implementer's proof and the funder's gate — the literal hinge of information-gates-money. The award is the funder's output and the implementer's input. The welfare.gap is authored once by the implementer and consumed twice — as its own outcome and as one term in the funder's portfolio impact.

4 · The funder side is essentially unbuilt — but rides reused rails

Unlike the implementer (need half live), the funder side is ADR-0072 — not yet built. But its internal modules are the same ERP stack already running; only the funding-specific objects and the cross-tenant grants are new:

  1. Provision the funder tenant(s) — MoHUA (national) / HUDD (state) on the jurisdiction ladder. ✗
  2. Govern the funding nouns — funding.program (with co-funding structure) + funding.award (signed, partial, stacking). ✗
  3. Encumbrance accounting — an award posts a commitment against the envelope; a tranche posts a release — both in the reused finance ledger. ◐ ledger exists
  4. Progress subscription — the funder's governed read-grant on a co-funded project's receipts (aggregator lane, already built, in reverse). ◐ lane exists
  5. Portfolio outcome rollup — aggregate the implementers' welfare.gap deltas across the funded portfolio → the scheme's value-for-money (needs the same prov.derivation attribution as the implementer side). ✗

The symmetry is the leverage: build the two shared objects (funding.award + the milestone-VC subscription) once, and both sides light up together.
